  • Standardized: each company's XBRL tags are mapped onto one template, so the lines are not the company's own presentation
  • As filed (SEC), the default: the complete balance sheet and income statement exactly as the company filed them in that 10-Q/10-K (its own lines, labels, and comparison columns), from the SEC's rendering of the filing
  • Others (in italics) = the section's subtotal minus the lines shown, so every section adds up and assets = liabilities + equity
  • % = share of total assets (balance sheet) or of revenue (income statement), for the selected quarter
  • Amounts in $ millions, except EPS; negative amounts in parentheses
  • Balance sheet = at the end of the quarter; income statement = for the three months of the quarter
  • Quarters = calendar quarters; the column header shows the exact period end
  • * = Q4 derived as annual (10-K) minus nine-month year-to-date; EPS is not derived this way, so it shows — for Q4
  • † = no total-liabilities line in the filing; shown as total liabilities and equity minus total equity
  • — = not reported by the company (e.g., airlines have no inventory or COGS)
  • Buttons under the quarter: the company's EDGAR page, the filing index (all documents in the filing), the 10-Q/10-K itself, and SEC's financial data viewer (every statement, note, and detail table)
  • Click a quarter heading to open that 10-Q/10-K on sec.gov; hover a line item, or press Show definitions, for what each line means
  • Switch company: dropdown, ◀ ▶, or Ctrl+← / Ctrl+→; switch quarter: Ctrl+↑ / Ctrl+↓

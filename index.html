Financial statements from SEC filings

43 US companies in 11 sectors, built from public SEC EDGAR filings (data.sec.gov) and refreshed weekly. For teaching introductory financial accounting and financial statement analysis.

Fundamentals →Every company's 10-Q and 10-K filings since 2024 by calendar quarter, with links to StockAnalysis, Finviz, and EDGAR.Ratios →Profit margins, current ratio, cash flows, shares, public float, P/E, and stock price, quarter by quarter since 2019; click a sector to compare its companies.Statements →The balance sheet and income statement of any company and quarter, exactly as filed with the SEC or standardized, with definitions for every line.
